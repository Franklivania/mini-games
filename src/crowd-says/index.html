Crowd Says!

Two teams. One board of the crowd's most popular answers. Guess them all before you rack up three strikes — or watch the other team steal the whole pot.

  1. Guess The team with the board guesses answers one at a time. Every hit adds its points to the pot.
  2. Strike Wrong guesses earn an ✕. Three strikes and the board goes to the other team.
  3. Steal The other team gets one guess. Hit, and they take the pot. Miss, and it goes back.
  4. Win Last round counts double. Highest score takes it.
